About Us

Welcome to PCEA Gatiiguru Church, a congregation of the Presbyterian Church of East Africa (PCEA), rooted in faith, community service, and the gospel of Jesus Christ.

About the Presbyterian Church of East Africa (PCEA)

The PCEA traces its roots to the Church of Scotland Mission in the 1890s. It is founded on Reformed theology, upholding the authority of Scripture, faith in Christ, grace alone, and the glory of God alone.

Mission: To Empower, Equip, Build & Transform God’s people for effective service through Preaching, Teaching & Witnessing in words & deeds.

Vision: To be a Great and Dynamic Godly Model Church for holistic Service in Pursuance of the Great Commission.
